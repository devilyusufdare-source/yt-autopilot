# Privacy policy — yt-autopilot

yt-autopilot is a personal automation tool. It is used by its owner only, to upload videos to the
owner's own YouTube channel on a schedule.

- It accesses YouTube only on behalf of the channel owner, using credentials the owner created.
- It stores no data about any other person. The only data it keeps are the video files it makes,
  their titles/descriptions and the public view counts of the owner's own videos.
- It does not sell, share or transmit any data to third parties.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
