# Outreach scripts (clients, in rupees and in dollars)

Three jobs we sell, in the order they pay off fastest. Payment: India → UPI to your own account;
abroad → Gumroad in Mom's name (card or the buyer's PayPal; pays her bank in INR).

## 1. Ready-made Shorts for small Indian channels (₹, UPI)

Who: the Indian channels in `content/prospects.md` (English-learning, study, psychology, GK). They
post Shorts, have 2K–20K subscribers, and are too small to afford an editor.

Price: ₹400 per Short, **₹3,000 for 10** (one week of posting). Delivery: 48 hours, vertical MP4,
voice + captions + footage, their title and description included.

Where to message: the channel's About page → email, or Instagram DM if listed. Attach one finished
video from the Releases page (CROSSPOST.md) as the sample. Five messages a day.

> Subject: 10 ready-to-post Shorts for [Channel name] — ₹3,000
>
> Hi [Name], I run an automated Shorts studio and I watched your channel ([one specific video title]).
> I make 30-second facts/explainer Shorts: voice, captions, footage, title and description, ready to
> upload. One is attached so you can see the quality.
>
> For [Channel name] I'd make 10 in your topic ([their topic]) for ₹3,000, delivered in 48 hours.
> If the first batch gets you views, we do a weekly bundle. Pay by UPI after you've seen the first two.
>
> Want me to make the first two now? Just reply with two topics.
>
> Yusuf

Follow-up after 4 days, one line: "Still happy to make the first two free — reply with two topics."
Never pay anyone, never share passwords, never upload to their channel yourself (send files).

## 2. One-page websites for local businesses (₹, UPI)

Who: businesses on Google Maps near you with **no website** (the card says so), or Instagram-only
shops: gyms, coaching centres, tutors, salons, bakeries, clinics, photographers.

Price: **₹5,000** one-time for a one-page site (photos, prices, WhatsApp button, map, Google reviews),
live on their own domain (they buy the domain, ₹700–1,000/year). Optional ₹500/month for updates.

> Hi, I searched for [category] in [area] and [Business] came up on Google Maps, but there's no
> website to look at. I make simple one-page sites for local businesses: photos, prices, a WhatsApp
> button, your Google reviews, your map. ₹5,000, ready in 2 days. Here are two examples:
> [demo links]. Want one for [Business]?

Start with people you know (a relative's shop, a tutor). The first two clients give the screenshots
and the reviews that bring the next ten.

## 3. Website redesigns for foreign small businesses ($, Gumroad)

Who: US/UK/Australian small businesses with a **bad** website (old, broken on a phone). Google Maps in
any mid-sized town → dentists, plumbers, gyms, restaurants → open the sites → the ugly ones have their
email on the contact page.

Price: **$250** for a one-page site, $300–600 for a redesign. Half upfront through Gumroad
("Website package" product), the rest when it's live. Twenty personalised emails a day at most, from
your own Gmail; more gets flagged as spam. Expect 1 reply in 30 and 1 client in 100 at the start.

> Subject: Your website on a phone (quick fix, flat price)
>
> Hi [Name], I looked at [business].com on my phone before writing: [the one specific problem you
> saw — "the menu doesn't open", "the text is tiny and the booking button is off-screen"]. About
> 70% of people who search for a [category] do it on a phone, so that's customers walking past.
>
> I build fast one-page sites for small businesses: your services, photos, reviews, a tap-to-call
> button, your map. Flat $250, live in 3 days, no monthly fees; you own everything. Two examples:
> [demo links].
>
> If you'd like, I'll send a free mock-up of your homepage first, no commitment.
>
> Yusuf

Scams to refuse, always: cheques or "overpayments", anything that asks you to pay a fee to get paid,
"clients" who want to send equipment or software to install.
